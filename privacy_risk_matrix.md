# Privacy Risk Matrix

Risk depends on context. These are educational examples, not universal ratings.

| Scenario | Likelihood | Impact | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public phone number | Medium | Medium | Exposure can enable unwanted contact or targeted scams |
| Real-time location exposure | Medium | High | Context and audience strongly affect impact |
| MFA disabled | Medium | High | Account-security weakness, not automatically a compromise |
| Public workplace detail | Medium | Medium | May provide context for social engineering |
| Old public posts | Medium | Medium | Historical information can remain discoverable |
| Unreviewed third-party apps | Medium | Medium | Permissions may exceed current needs |
